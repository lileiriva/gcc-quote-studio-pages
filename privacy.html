Privacy

Privacy Policy

Draft for review · Last updated October 19, 2026

In short: GCC Quote Studio is designed to keep your business information and documents on your device. The app does not currently operate an account system or send your quote data to a developer server.

Information you enter

Business profile details, quote templates, drafts, customer details, and saved logo or stamp images are stored locally in the app on your device. GCC Quote Studio does not transmit this content to us.

Documents and on-device analysis

When you choose a PDF or image to create a template, the app extracts text for review using Apple system frameworks. The original document is used for that import review and is not retained as an uploaded document by GCC Quote Studio. If an Apple-provided language model is available, it may be used by the app to suggest a business name. The app has no developer-operated document processing service.

Sharing and backups

You can create a backup file and choose where to save or share it using the iOS share sheet. Once you share or save a file outside the app, the destination service or recipient handles it under its own terms. Device backups may also include app data according to your Apple account and device settings.

Diagnostics and tracking

The current app build does not include advertising tracking, analytics, or a developer-operated crash reporting service. This may change in a future version; this policy will be updated before any material change in data practices.

Retention and deletion

Your saved data remains on your device until you delete it in the app or remove the app. Files you export, share, or include in device backups are outside the app and may need to be deleted separately.

Children

GCC Quote Studio is a business productivity app and is not designed for children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app changes. For privacy questions, contact lileicodex@gmail.com.
