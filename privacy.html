Privacy Policy

Draft for review · Last updated October 19, 2026

In short: GCC Quote Studio stores saved business and quotation data locally in the app. The app does not operate an account system, analytics, advertising tracking, or a developer-operated cloud service. Data leaves the app only when you choose to export or share it, or when you contact support by email.

Who is responsible

Data controller: lileiriva. For privacy questions or requests, email lileicodex@gmail.com.

Information stored in the app

The app stores the business profile you enter, quotation templates, quote drafts, and related customer details such as names, attention contacts, addresses, and tax numbers. It also stores logo and stamp images you add. This information is used to prepare, save, and export quotations. Saved app data is kept in the app’s local storage on your device; the app does not send it to a developer-operated server.

Documents and analysis

When you select a PDF or image to create a template, Apple’s Vision framework extracts text and the app creates temporary page previews for the review screen. The selected original document, extracted text, and previews are used for that import review and are not copied into the saved template. The source file name may be saved with a template. The values you confirm, such as a business name, tax number, and VAT rate, are saved as part of that template.

On compatible devices running iOS 26 or later, the app may use Apple’s on-device Foundation Model to suggest a business name. It passes up to the first 5,000 characters of extracted text to that on-device model. The current app implementation does not send this text to a developer server or a third-party model provider.

PDFs, backups, and sharing

If you export or share a quotation PDF, it can contain business and customer details shown in that quotation. A JSON backup contains saved templates, quote drafts, business profile information, logos and stamps, sent-quote image snapshots, and app recovery copies. Review the backup before sharing it because it may contain personal or tax information.

Exports and backups are handed to the destination you select in the iOS share sheet, such as Files, email, or a messaging app. The selected destination and its provider handle the file under their own terms. Apple device backups may also include app data according to your device and Apple account settings.

Support email

If you contact support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include. They are used to respond to your request and troubleshoot the app. The support mailbox uses Gmail, so Google processes that correspondence under its own terms and privacy policy. Please do not include customer names, tax numbers, or unredacted quotation documents unless they are necessary for your request.

Retention and deletion

Saved templates, drafts, profile details, and images remain in the app until you delete them or remove the app from your device. Data in device backups and files you exported or shared are separate copies and may need to be deleted separately. Support email is retained only as needed to respond and maintain reasonable support records. To ask about a support email or privacy request, contact us at the address above; we cannot remotely access or delete data stored only on your device.

Children and changes

The app is a business productivity tool and is not designed for children. We will update this policy if the app’s data practices change. The current version and effective date will be shown on this page.
