Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Your templates, quote drafts, business profile, logo, and stamp are stored locally in the app.

How do I move data to another device?
Use New → Back Up Data, save the JSON backup somewhere you control, then choose Restore Data on the other device. The backup may contain customer and tax details; keep it private.

Can I share a quotation?
Open a quote, preview or export its PDF, then use the iOS share sheet to send or save it.
